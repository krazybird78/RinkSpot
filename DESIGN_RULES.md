# Legal & Design Compliance Guidelines

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **STRICT COMPLIANCE REQUIRED**: RinkSpot does NOT hold licenses for NHL, PWHL, or any professional sports team logos/trademarks.

## 1. Copyright & Licensing Rules

### 🚫 STRICTLY PROHIBITED (Zero Tolerance)
- **Logos**: NEVER use official team logos (e.g., the Canadiens 'CH', the Bruins 'B', the Golden Knights helmet).
- **Team Names**: Do not use trademarked full team names (e.g., "Golden Knights", "Maple Leafs").
- **Exact Replicas**: Do not copy proprietary jersey artwork.

### ✅ APPROVED ASSETS
- **Colors**: Team color palettes are permitted (e.g., "Vegas Gold & Black", "Blue, White & Red").
- **City Names**: Use "Las Vegas", "Montreal", "Toronto", "Boston".
- **Generic Patterns**: Standard striping patterns and blocks.

## 2. Avatar Design Standards

### Visual Style
- **Format**: Modern "ID Card" (Head & Shoulders / Bust).
- **Aesthetic**: Clean, Vector/Illustration, "Mobile Game Pro" quality.
- **Background**: Outdoor Rink (Ice, Trees, Winter Sky) - No Sci-Fi elements.

### Character Equipment
- **Jersey**: Must be worn **OVER** shoulder pads.
- **Silhouette**: Broad, hockey-tough silhouette.
- **Helmet**: Generic pro helmet (no decals).

### Typography
- **Primary Text**: CITY NAME ONLY (e.g., "LAS VEGAS").
- **Secondary Text**: "Player ID", "Manage Profile".
- **No Team Brands**: If in doubt, leave it out.
